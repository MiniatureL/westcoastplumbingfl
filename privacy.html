Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how West Coast Plumbing FL (the “Website”), an independently operated plumbing information and lead-generation website, may collect, use, and share information when you visit westcoastplumbingfl.com or call the telephone number displayed on the Website.

The Website helps visitors connect with independent service providers. The Website operator does not necessarily perform the plumbing services discussed here.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Website or its telephone referral services, information may include:

  • Information you provide: your name, contact details, address or general service location, and details you choose to share about a plumbing issue or project.
  • Telephone information: the number you call from, caller ID details when available, the date and time of a call, call duration, and routing information.
  • Inquiry details: information you provide during a telephone conversation, such as the type of service requested, preferred appointment timing, and relevant property details.
  • Basic technical information: information that website hosting and security systems may process, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and technical log data.

Information collected will depend on the systems and service providers actually used. The current supplied homepage has no inquiry form.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, help connect callers with relevant independent service providers, facilitate a requested service consultation, maintain and improve website performance and inquiry quality, help prevent fraud or misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This Website operates for lead-generation and referral purposes. Calling the number shown on the Website may connect you with, or result in a referral to, an independent third-party plumbing business or other relevant service provider. The Website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring a qualifying inquiry.

Information reasonably needed to handle your inquiry may be provided to a relevant service provider. Each independent provider is responsible for its own work, communications, and privacy practices. Contacting the Website does not, by itself, authorize unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Routing, Tracking, and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be routed or tracked through third-party telecommunications providers to connect callers with service providers and help manage inquiries. If call tracking is used, related details may include calling number, time, duration, and referral or routing information.

Call recording has not been confirmed for this Website. If recording is introduced or used by a participating service, callers must receive appropriate notice and consent must be obtained where required by applicable law. This policy does not claim that every call is recorded.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The supplied homepage does not include an identified analytics platform, advertising pixel, or cookie-consent system. Depending on the actual hosting setup, essential technical processes may still be used for website delivery, security, and operation. If optional analytics, advertising cookies, or similar technologies are added, this section should be updated to describe those practices and any choices available to visitors.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with relevant independent service providers to address your request and with technical vendors that support website hosting, telephone routing, or other necessary operations. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or to protect against misuse. The scope of sharing depends on the Website's actual operating arrangements.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect personal information, but no website or communications system can guarantee absolute security. Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including legitimate operational or legal needs. No fixed retention period is represented here because one has not been verified.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the information held, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You can also choose not to provide optional details during an inquiry. Independent providers may need to address requests concerning information they collect or hold directly.

For a privacy request, use the Website's published contact channel where available and identify the information or telephone inquiry involved. A dedicated privacy contact address or request process has not yet been supplied and should be added before publishing this policy.

The Website may refer you to independent service providers or third-party websites. Their privacy notices, information practices, terms, and services may differ from those described here. Review their policies before providing information directly to them.

This policy may be updated when the Website's features, service relationships, or information-handling practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
